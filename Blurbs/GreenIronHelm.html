Perfect armor-wear for the nature-loving monster, this Green Iron Helm is the color of a forest in summer. Best of all, it'll hide you well when travelling in jungle terrain. Maybe even better than best of all, it provides protection Level 3 during combat. That might even be enough to keep your head from being cracked open like a green coconut. You can carry two.
