Ever had a day when you simply didn't have the energy you needed to get your chores done? You needed one more trip through the Large Quarry but ran smack-dab out of time? Well here's the elixir for you - Sparky Lotus Elixir. It's easy to make, just mix 6 White Lotus Elixirs and a Spark Sprig in your Voodoo Cauldron for a quick 18 Action Points and Voíla you've got one. To use it, USE it and you'll find yourself with the energy equivalent of an additional 150 Action Points. You can use it at any point during a turn and the additional Action Points will last for that turn ONLY. Next turn, your energy levels will be back to normal. Oh, yeah, don't forget to add one oil during its making.
